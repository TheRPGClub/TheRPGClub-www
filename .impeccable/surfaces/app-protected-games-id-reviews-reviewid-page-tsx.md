## Job
A member writes or edits their review of a game: title, overall score, an optional scorecard, and the story, seeing the story exactly as it will be published.

## Direction contract
THESIS: the review is a sheet being proofed on a board, with the scores as notes taped in its margin. It refuses the boxed form field.
OWN-WORLD: the key-art banner with the title written in Anton over it, the printer's-ruler sliders, the yellow pencil ring on the score and on a pressed tool, the taped tilted notes, crop marks.
FIRST VIEWPORT: the banner with the title, the toolbar, the opening of the story, and the Overall note.
